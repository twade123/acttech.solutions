Trevor.

Trevor runs AI agents. Its Google connection is in development and not yet available: nobody can sign in to Trevor with Google today. This page lists each Google service the connection is being built for, what each permission is for, and the exact permissions Trevor plans to ask for.

Where your Google data goes. When Trevor's agents read Google data, they send it to Anthropic, an AI model provider, for processing. Your data does not stay only on your own computer.

Details: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

How your Google data will move

  1. Google

    You will sign in with Google and choose which of the services below to give Trevor access to.

  2. Trevor

    Trevor runs on your own Mac. Running it for a company, on our server that the company's people reach through Trevor's cockpit, is planned and not yet available.

  3. Anthropic

    When Trevor's agents read Google data, they send it to Anthropic's AI models for processing.

Google's Limited Use requirements
